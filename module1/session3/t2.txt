Ques-2: What is the difference between IPv4 and IPv6? Answer in 2 lines.
        ----------------------------------------------------------------

Ans: IPv4 uses 32-bit addresses allowing about 4.3 billion unique addresses, whereas IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses providing a vastly larger     
     
     address space. IPv6 also includes improved features such as simplified headers, auto-configuration, and better support for security 
     
     and mobility.
