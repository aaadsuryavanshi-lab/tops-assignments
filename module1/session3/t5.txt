Ques-5: Explain DNS hierarchy (root, TLD, domain, subdomain) with www.google.com as an example.
        ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Ans: The DNS hierarchy consists of several levels, starting from the root level down to the subdomain level. Here’s a breakdown:

    *Root Level: The topmost level of the DNS hierarchy, represented by a dot (.) or an empty string. It points to the authoritative   
     ----------
     servers for the top-level domains (TLDs). 


    *Top-Level Domains (TLDs): These are the first level below the root and help classify websites based on purpose or organization type. 
     ------------------------
     For Examples include .com, .org, and .gov. 


    *Second-Level Domains: These are the main domain names registered by organizations. 
     --------------------
     For example, in "www.google.com", "google" is the second-level domain. 

    *Subdomains: Extensions of the main domain used for organization. 
     ----------
     For example, "www" is a subdomain of "google".
 

    *Hostnames: Identifies specific servers or devices within a domain. 
     ---------
     For example, "mail.google.com" is a hostname within the "google" domain.
