Ques: Explain in your own words the difference between encapsulation and abstraction, using examples from any social media app you use daily (like    
      Instagram or WhatsApp).

Ans: 
----
Encapsulation means hiding data and allowing access to it through specific methods.

Example – Instagram:
Your phone number or password is kept private. You cannot directly change it; you use proper settings or methods to update it.

Abstraction means hiding the internal working and showing only the important features to the user.

Example – WhatsApp:
When you send a message, you simply click the Send button. You don't need to know how the message is processed and delivered in the background.

In simple words:
Encapsulation → Hides data and controls access to it.
Abstraction → Hides complex working and shows only what is needed.

Example to remember:
Instagram's private user information = Encapsulation
WhatsApp's simple "Send Message" feature = Abstraction.
